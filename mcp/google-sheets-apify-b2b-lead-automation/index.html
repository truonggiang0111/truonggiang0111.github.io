2. Copy-Paste Zero-Key Custom Formulas ($0, Zero API Tokens)

These three custom formulas execute live via Google's UrlFetchApp.fetch() directly against public endpoints — no Apify key or paid API required:

A. =DECODE_GNEWS_URL(urlOrRange) — Decode Single Links or Entire Columns

Solves both legacy CBMi protobuf Base64 links offline and modern encrypted AU_yqL links via DotsSplashUi Fbv4je. You can also highlight cells in Google Sheets and click B2B Market Intel -> Decode Selected Google News URLs to overwrite or write decoded URLs in bulk.

Google Sheets Formula — Single Cell or Spill Range 
=DECODE_GNEWS_URL(A2)
=DECODE_GNEWS_URL(A2:A25)

B. =UK_GOV_TENDERS(keyword, limit) — Live UK Contracts Finder OCDS Table

Pulls fresh public procurement notices from contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Published/Notices/OCDS/Search (and REST v2 fallback), spilling a formatted header row plus live records with columns [OCID, Title, Buyer, ValueGBP, Deadline, NoticeURL].

Google Sheets Formula — Live UK Gov Tenders 
=UK_GOV_TENDERS("software", 15)
=UK_GOV_TENDERS("cloud", 25)

C. =CLINICAL_TRIALS_LEADS(condition, phase, limit) — Recruiting Biotech Sponsor Emails

Queries clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies for actively recruiting Phase 1–3 Industry trials and spills a 7-column lead table: [NCT, BriefTitle, Sponsor, Phase, Status, CentralContactName, CentralContactEmail].

Google Sheets Formula — Recruiting ClinicalTrials.gov Leads 
=CLINICAL_TRIALS_LEADS("oncology", "PHASE2,PHASE3", 15)
=CLINICAL_TRIALS_LEADS("CAR-T", "Phase 1", 10)
