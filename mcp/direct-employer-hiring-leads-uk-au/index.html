1. Reed.co.uk: Stripping `~48%` Bootcamp Pollution + Detecting Outside IR35

When B2B lead-gen pipelines scrape Reed.co.uk without strict server and detail-level hygiene, outreach lists fill up with recruitment agencies and paid "guaranteed job placement" training courses:

  • directEmployerOnly: true (direct=True + Detail Verification): Enforces Reed's server-side direct-employer parameter and verifies detail-page advertiser metadata to strip third-party staffing agencies.
  • excludeTrainingCourses: true (hideTrainingJobs=true): Rejects paid bootcamp/course listings flagged via isTrainingJob — which pollute ~48% of unfiltered direct postings on Reed.

2. Seek.com.au & Seek.co.nz: Bypassing HTML `403` Blocks via v5 JSON + GraphQL

Why HTML scrapers fail on Seek: Fetching https://www.seek.com.au/job/<id> HTML triggers Kasada anti-bot challenges (HTTP 403), forcing traditional scrapers to rent expensive residential proxies and headless browsers.

ttyan0111/seek-au-nz-jobs-hiring-signals eliminates browser overhead completely by chaining two native first-party endpoints over got-scraping:

  1. Search Layer (GET /api/jobsearch/v5/search): Queries AU-Main (seek.com.au) or NZ-Main (seek.co.nz) with native server-side remote filtering (workarrangement=3 when remoteOnly: true).
  2. Company Signal Enrichment (POST /graphql): Queries jobDetails(id: $id) and companyProfile(zone: "anz-1") to extract companyWebsite, companySize (headcount bracket), companyIndustry, isVerifiedAdvertiser, and clean plain-text job descriptions.
  3. Multi-Signal Agency Rejection (directEmployerOnly: true): Flags and removes staffing agencies by checking companyProfile.overview.industry (Employment Services), Seek classifications (Recruitment - Agency), advertiser brand heuristics, and "on behalf of our client" copy.
